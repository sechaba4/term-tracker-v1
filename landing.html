September Planner

Drag a slider — see what Sep mark you need in each module to reach 50% overall. Know your target before you open the textbook.

AI Exam Grader

Paste a question, memo, and your answer. Claude grades it against CTA marking rubrics and shows exactly which marks you'd lose and why.

Study Schedule

Week-by-week plan from the official PGDA programme timetable. Tick periods as complete — progress persists in your browser.

Task Tracker

Individual module tasks per week. Filter by FAC, AUC, TAX, or MAF — check them off and see progress at a glance.

Question Breakdown

Enter earned vs. available marks per question. Colour-coded bars and running totals show exactly where marks leaked — per module, per test.

Performance Insights

Trajectory chart, trend indicators, priority flags for at-risk modules, and automatically generated study recommendations based on your actual marks.
